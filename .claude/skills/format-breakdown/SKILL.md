# format-breakdown

One proven post, turned into a format.

## Inputs

- The post the user picked (link). Its transcript or slides if available.

## Output

- **Format name:** short and plain.
- **Example:** the link, plus the hook and script or slides verbatim.
- **Formula:** beat by beat, what each part does.
- **Why it works:** the attention and retention mechanics, in 2–3 lines.
- **What stays, what changes:** what to keep every time, and what to swap for the user's topic.

## Rules

- Break down only what's actually in the post. Never pad the formula.
