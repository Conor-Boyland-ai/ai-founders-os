# lead-magnet-script

One free guide that solves one problem and points to the offer.

## Inputs (from the brief)

- The promise (what they'll be able to do) and the keyword that delivers it.
- The user's material: the steps, prompts, screenshots and proof.
- **Model (optional):** a past lead magnet or a framework to model. It sets structure only, never wording.

## Output

- **Title:** the promise, plainly.
- **Intro:** short. Who it's for and what they'll have when they finish.
- **Steps:** straight value, one step at a time. Exact prompts in full. Screenshots marked `[SCREENSHOT: what it shows]`.
- **Bridge:** a short close that connects the result to the offer, with one CTA.

## Rules

- Value first. The pitch is a short bridge at the end, never the point.
- Never invent steps, results or prompts the user hasn't given. Flag gaps as `[GAP: what's missing]`.
