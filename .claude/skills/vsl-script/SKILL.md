# vsl-script

One spoken sales video for one offer. It's watched after a DM conversation, and its job is to get the viewer to DM back to start.

## Inputs (from the brief)

- The offer, and the user's material: notes, story, proof, method, deliverables, guarantee.
- **Framework (optional):** a different formula to use instead of the default.
- **Model (optional):** a finished VSL to model. It sets structure only, never wording.

## The default formula

1. **Open + big promise:** who it's for, where they're stuck, and the result the user gets them.
2. **Credibility:** their experience, plus 2–3 client results with real numbers. Name the method.
3. **Problem/solution:** what most people do wrong and why it fails, then how the method does the opposite and gets results.
4. **Method:** the method step by step, with the steps or results shown on screen.
5. **Proof:** their own results and/or the best client results, with before and after.
6. **Offer details:** timeframe, deliverables, promised results.
7. **Qualify:** who the user works with, and who the user does NOT work with.
8. **Risk removal:** answer the biggest objections; a results-based guarantee.
9. **CTA:** DM back with in, out, or any questions.

## Output

The full spoken script, word for word, marked by step. On-screen text noted only where a step calls for it.

## Rules

- Write for the ear: how the user talks out loud.
- Never invent proof, testimonials, prices or guarantees. Flag gaps as `[GAP: what's missing]`.
