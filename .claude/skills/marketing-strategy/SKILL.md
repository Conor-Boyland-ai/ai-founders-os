# marketing-strategy

A simple strategy every other agent can read. You ask, the user answers, you shape it.

## Before you start

Read what's already in the user's Notion brain (DNA, Offers, Marketing). Skip any question it already answers, and show them what you found instead.

## The interview

Ask ONE question at a time. Give a short example answer with each question so they know what you mean. Use their exact words; never polish them into marketing speak.

1. **Your buyer:** who is the one person you help? What do they do, and where are they stuck right now?
2. **Their pain:** what keeps them up at night? What have they already tried that didn't work?
3. **What they want:** if this was solved, what would their life or business look like?
4. **What stops them buying:** what do they believe that keeps them from getting help? (For example "I can figure it out myself" or "I can't afford it.") For each one, ask the user how they'd answer it. That answer is the reframe.
5. **Your promise:** in one sentence, what do you help them get?
6. **Your content pillars:** 3 topics you'll keep posting about, each one tied to their pain or what they want.
7. **Your path to a sale:** daily reels bring people to a free guide, the free guide gets them on your email list, and daily emails and stories sell your offer. Fill in their free guide and their offer.

If the user has real buyer words (DMs, comments, emails, call notes), ask them to paste them in. If Gmail is connected, offer to pull them with the buyer-research skill. Real words beat guesses.

## Output

Show the finished strategy on one screen:
- **Buyer:** who they are, their pain, what they want, in their own words
- **What stops them buying:** each belief, plus the reframe
- **Promise:** one sentence
- **Content pillars:** 3
- **Path:** reel → free guide → emails and stories → offer

## Save it

Once the user says it's right, save **Buyer** (buyer, pain, wants, beliefs and reframes) as a page in **DNA**, and **Strategy** (promise, pillars, path) as a page in **Marketing**. Tell them every agent will now write and research from it.

## Rules

- Never invent a buyer detail, pain or belief. If the user doesn't know yet, mark it `[TO LEARN]` and suggest how to find out.
- Keep it simple. This is a starting strategy, and it gets sharper as they sell.
