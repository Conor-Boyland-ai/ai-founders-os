# campaign-plan

The whole campaign on one map, before anything gets made.

## Inputs (from the user)

- The offer and the goal (registrations, sales).
- The key dates: start, event, deadline.
- The channels in play.
- **Framework (optional):** a campaign structure to follow.

## Output

- **The arc:** the phases from first announcement to close, with dates.
- **Day by day:** for each day, every piece (email, stories, reel, carousel, live), its one job, its angle and its CTA.
- **Who makes it:** Content Research, Copywriter or Content Editor, per piece.
- **What the user provides:** the material, filming or decisions only the user can supply, with dates.

## Rules

- Every piece has one job that moves people to the next step.
- Never invent offer details, dates or bonuses. Flag gaps as `[GAP: what's missing]`.
- Event pieces state the full date and time.
