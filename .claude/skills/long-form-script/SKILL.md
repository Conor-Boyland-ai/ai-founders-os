# long-form-script

One long piece built around one big idea.

## Inputs (from the brief)

- The big idea and the user's material for it.
- Where it's going (newsletter, article, video) and the CTA.
- **Model:** a past piece or framework to model. It sets structure only, never wording.

## Output

- **Headline:** 3 options.
- **Intro:** the hook and why it matters to the reader now.
- **Body:** sections in the model's order, each doing the job its counterpart does in the model.
- **Close:** the takeaway and one CTA.

## Rules

- One big idea. Every section moves it forward.
- Never invent stories, facts or results. Flag gaps as `[GAP: what's missing]`.
