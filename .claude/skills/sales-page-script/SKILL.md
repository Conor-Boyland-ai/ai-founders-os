# sales-page-script

One sales page for one offer.

## Inputs (from the brief)

- The offer, and the user's material: notes, proof, price, deadline, bonuses, guarantee.
- **Framework (optional):** a different formula to use instead of the default.
- **Model (optional):** a finished sales page to model. It sets structure only, never wording.

## The default formula (Painkiller)

1. **Above the fold:** pre-header calling out the one person; headline "Use [Solution] to Get [Result] Without [Pain]"; subheadline with more benefits or proof; product mockup; buy button with a context-specific CTA (never "buy now").
2. **Pain:** one painful, specific headline line; pace their pain so they feel seen, not judged; transition promising why it's happening and how to fix it.
3. **Diagnosis:** a one-line headline that lands like a loving slap; diagnose the problem fast (a metaphor if it fits); the cost of inaction; transition to a better way.
4. **Solution:** a one-line headline that flows from the diagnosis; the solution as the obvious next step; benefit bullets (what they stop doing, start doing); transition to what happened when the user used it.
5. **Result:** a one-line headline that makes the result real; proof (screenshots, numbers, client results); future pace: this is the only thing missing.
6. **Creator story + credibility:** who the user is, only what's relevant; the problem the user overcame with the same solution; one impressive result.
7. **Pitch:** introduce the offer with the problem, solution and transformation again; bullets of what they get.
8. **Price:** anchor (the real value of the result, or the cost of the problem); the price today.
9. **Bonuses:** 1–3, each valuable enough to sell alone, each anchored then slashed.
10. **Social proof:** testimonials.
11. **Guarantee:** the timeframe and the promise.
12. **Crossroads close:** "Right now you have two options." Option 1 re-opens the pain; option 2 is buying and getting the result. Final CTA and sign-off.
13. **FAQ:** the most common objections, answered.

## Output

The page section by section, each headed by its section name, with every CTA written out in full.

## Rules

- Never invent proof, testimonials, prices, dates, bonuses or guarantees. Flag gaps as `[GAP: what's missing]`.
