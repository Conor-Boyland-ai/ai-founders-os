# email-script

One email, written to one person, about one idea. For a sequence, write each email with this skill, in the order the brief sets.

## Inputs (from the brief)

- The idea and the user's material for it.
- What it's promoting, if anything.
- **Model (optional):** a past email, sequence or framework to model. It sets structure only, never wording.

## The formula

- **Subject:** offer 3 short options, each giving the reader a clear reason to open. Keep them congruent with the email.
- **Body:** deliver the one idea like you're talking to a single reader. Keep paragraphs short and put line breaks between them so it stays scannable. Bold a key line where it helps the point land — use it where it earns its place, not everywhere.
- **Offer:** when there's something to sell, introduce it in the reader's terms and end with one clear CTA on its own line.
- **PS (optional):** a short line that adds a new angle or points back to the offer.

## Rules

- The author's material (sales pages, scripts, notes) supplies the meaning and feeling — write fresh sentences that deliver them.
- Leave prices out of emails and send readers to the sales page for them. The one exception is a super-cheap offer, where the low price is itself the hook.
- Never invent facts, proof, or offer details. Anything missing gets a flagged gap (e.g. `[GAP: need the link]`).
- Event emails spell out the full date AND time, sign-off included.
- Length is whatever the idea needs.
